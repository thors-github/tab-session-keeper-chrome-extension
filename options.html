How the rules work

A rule matches the same protocol, host and port. Subdomains are separate. The extension does not open tabs or reload pages. Unloaded or frozen tabs wait until their document can run.

A tab exception takes priority over the website rule. It ends when the tab closes or Chrome restarts.

Recognized BeyondTrust consoles use activity signals. Guacamole and Proxmox VE use their own session-renewal interfaces. The popup shows the actual interval; your saved interval stays unchanged. A custom keep-alive URL uses background requests at your saved interval.

Successful requests, activity signals and renewal attempts do not guarantee an active login. The website decides whether to extend a session; fixed session limits still apply. Nothing runs while Chrome is closed or the device is asleep.
